Privacy Policy

Trust Built Roofing Co. (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a roof estimate request, we collect the details you provide: ZIP code, property and roof information, your name, phone number, email address, optional notes, and any photos you attach.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your estimate request, schedule an inspection, and follow up about your roofing project.

Contact consent (TCPA)

By submitting the form and checking the consent box, you agree that Trust Built Roofing Co. may call, text, or email you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP or contacting us at info@trustbuiltroofing.com.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with team members and service providers needed to fulfill your request.
