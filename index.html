Roofing 101

Your Roofing Questions, Answered

It's necessary to replace a roof if it's past its average lifetime (20 years or less) or if you notice any water damage. Other signs it's time to replace your roof include:

  • Loose or missing roofing material
  • Broken or deformed roofing
  • Sagging ceiling
  • Increase in energy bills
  • Pest infestation

Roof replacement cost depends on your home's size, roof pitch, materials, and current roof condition. Trust Built Roofing Co. provides an estimate after reviewing the property details.

A durable, professionally installed roof can improve curb appeal, help protect the structure, and support long-term home value.

Installation timing depends on the roof size, scope of work, material choice, weather, and jobsite conditions.
